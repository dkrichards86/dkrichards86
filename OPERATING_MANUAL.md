# Operating Manual

This document exists to help us work better together. It's not a list of demands — it's a guide to how I operate, where I excel, and where I have blind spots.

## Communication

Default to written communication. I prefer Slack for most things and I'm a zero-inboxer — you'll typically get a fast response. For urgent or genuinely complex topics, a call is better. Keep it succinct and to-the-point; I value efficiency over pleasantries.

**Timing:**
- I'm on the East Coast (ET)
- 4:30–8:00 PM ET: limited availability (family time)
- Mornings are my best deep work hours — peak focus around 10 AM ET

## Work Rhythm

Mornings are when I do my best deep work. Afternoons are meeting time — that's fine, but give me a bit of breathing room between meetings to shift focus. Back-to-back marathons drain me.

I prefer solo time to think through options, then collaborative time to review. Extended pairing sessions or group whiteboarding aren't my best format — I lose focus in those.

## Feedback

I'm direct because I come from a military background and I think directness is a form of respect. Tell me what I need to know when I need to know it. No sugarcoating required.

I'm growth-minded. Feedback — even negative feedback — is an opportunity to improve. Don't hesitate to be honest with me. When I give feedback, I make it actionable and explain the "why" behind the "what."

## What I Need From You

- **From my manager:** Clarity. Point me in a direction and I'll figure out the details. I value autonomy.
- **From everyone:** Come prepared. My biggest pet peeve is lack of preparation in meetings. I show up prepared and I expect the same.
- **From our time together:** Something actionable — answers, new questions, or clear next steps.

## What I'm Great At

- **Systems thinking.** I think through second-order effects and make decisions that align with longer-term strategy rather than quick fixes.
- **Complex problem-solving.** My best days are when I can dig into a hard problem, understand it deeply, and pitch a well-reasoned solution.
- **Correctness and accuracy.** I push for precision and clarity in technical discussions.

## Where I Have Blind Spots

- **Expectation management.** I set a high bar for myself and sometimes unfairly expect others to match it without considering their context. I'm actively working on this — if I'm being unreasonable, call me out.
- **Routine work drains me.** CRUD work and UI projects sap my energy. I'll do them when needed, but I won't bring my best energy.
- **Impatience with incorrectness.** If someone shares something that's flat-out wrong, it frustrates me more than it probably should.

## How to Get the Best From Me

- Give me complex, ambiguous problems that need careful thought
- Respect my focus time in the mornings
- Come to meetings prepared
- Be direct — I can handle hard truths
- Schedule a call for deep discussions rather than hashing it out over Slack
- Value output and action over process and ceremony
